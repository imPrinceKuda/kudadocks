4. About this website

This site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript written for KudaDocks. It uses no third-party code, fonts, analytics or trackers. Text is shown in your device's own system font. The demo icons are original drawings.

5. Accessibility

We want KudaDocks and this website to be usable by everyone, and we aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. The site works with a keyboard, including the dock demos (use the arrow keys to choose an item, and Alt with an arrow key to move it), labels its controls for screen readers, and calms its animations if your device asks for reduced motion.

If something is hard to use, email contact@kudaxd.com and tell us what you were trying to do. We'll work on a fix.

If you believe something on this site or in KudaDocks infringes your copyright or trademark, or you have any other legal concern, email contact@kudaxd.com with the details and how to reach you. We'll respond promptly.
