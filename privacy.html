Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

The short version: KudaDocks does not collect your personal information. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you set up stays on your PC. A few optional features talk to outside services, and this page lists each one.

1. Who we are

KudaDocks is made by Kuda ("we", "us"). This policy covers the KudaDocks desktop app and this website. Contact: contact@kudaxd.com.

2. The KudaDocks app

We do not collect personal data through the app. KudaDocks has no user account, sends no usage statistics, crash reports or analytics to us, and shows no ads. We have no server that receives information from the app.

Information that stays on your PC

To work as a taskbar, KudaDocks reads information on your computer and keeps some of it in the folder %AppData%\KudaDocks. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • What it reads to show your dock: open windows and their titles, app names and icons, notification-area icons, app shortcuts and recent-file lists that Windows keeps for each app, music playback details from Windows' media controls, and system readings such as processor, memory, network, disk and battery use.
  • What it stores: your settings and pinned apps, calendar events, to-dos, notes, timers, the subscriptions, payouts and AI limits you type in, and a local error log used for troubleshooting.
  • Screen time: if screen time is on (it is by default and can be turned off in settings), KudaDocks notes which app is in front every few seconds and keeps daily totals. If you separately turn on website tracking (off by default), it also records the page titles shown in your browser's window. Screen time data never leaves your PC, and you can clear it in settings.
  • Earbuds battery: if you add this widget, KudaDocks listens for nearby Bluetooth signals that report battery levels. It does not connect to or pair with any device.

3. Optional features that use the internet

Some features need to fetch information from outside services. Each one only connects when you use that feature. As with any internet connection, the service receives your IP address and basic technical details of the request. These services are run by third parties under their own privacy policies.

FeatureWhat is sent, and to whom
Weather widgetsThe city you type in settings is sent once to Open-Meteo to find its location. Forecasts are then requested from Open-Meteo or the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway) using that location.
Currency widgetDownloads a table of exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API or Frankfurter. No personal information is included. Rates are kept for about 12 hours.
Calendar linksIf you add the private calendar link from Google Calendar, Outlook, iCloud or another service, KudaDocks downloads that link to show your events. The link is stored only on your PC. Treat it like a password.
Voice chat widgetOnly if you add it and choose Connect. It talks to the Discord app running on your PC, which asks for your permission. Discord's own services complete the sign-in and supply profile pictures. The sign-in token is kept in memory only, never saved.
Launcher web searchWhen you choose to search the web, your default browser opens your search engine of choice with the words you typed.

KudaDocks does not currently check for updates on its own. If a future version does, this policy will be updated first.

4. This website

  • No cookies, no tracking. This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics, ads or social media plugins, and loads no fonts or scripts from other companies. Its demos run entirely in your browser.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the installer is downloaded from GitHub. GitHub, Inc. receives your IP address and request details when you visit or download, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive those records.
  • Links. Links to other websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are their own.
  • Email. If you email us, we use your message only to reply, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

5. Your rights and choices

Because we don't collect personal data, we hold nothing about you to access, correct or delete. We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are used in the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

You stay in control of what the app keeps on your PC:

  • Turn off screen time, or clear it, in the app's settings.
  • Remove any widget that uses the internet, and the connection stops.
  • Uninstall KudaDocks from Settings, then Apps, then Installed apps. To remove your saved settings too, delete the folder %AppData%\KudaDocks.

If you live in a region with data protection laws, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California, you may contact us with any privacy question and we will respond as the law requires.

6. Children

KudaDocks and this website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the release notes of the next version of the app.
