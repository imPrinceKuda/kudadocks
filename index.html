Questions

Is KudaDocks really free?

Yes. There's no price, no trial, no account and no ads.

What happens to the Windows taskbar?

KudaDocks hides it while it runs and shows its own dock instead. Quit KudaDocks, or uninstall it, and the Windows taskbar comes straight back.

Do I need administrator rights?

No. KudaDocks installs for your Windows account only.

Does it work on Windows 10?

KudaDocks is built for Windows 11. It may run on recent versions of Windows 10, but it isn't tested there.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Settings, then Apps, then Installed apps, find KudaDocks and choose Uninstall. Your settings are kept in %AppData%\KudaDocks; delete that folder if you want them gone too.

Does KudaDocks collect my data?

No. It doesn't send us anything, and there are no analytics or ads. The Privacy Policy lists the few optional features that connect to outside services, such as weather.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?

Windows SmartScreen warns about new apps until enough people have downloaded them. Choose More info, then Run anyway. You can check the file's fingerprint in the download section above.

Is KudaDocks made by Microsoft?

No. KudaDocks is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Microsoft.
